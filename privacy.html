PRIVACY AND DATA PROTECTION POLICY

REGULATORY COMPLIANCE (GDPR) | LAST REVISED: JUNE 2026

C&D Web Studios assumes as a fundamental commitment the safeguarding of privacy and the protection of personal data of all Users interacting with our digital ecosystem. This policy is designed in strict compliance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation - GDPR) and the applicable national laws in Portugal.

1. Data Controller

The entity responsible for collecting and processing personal data is C&D Web Studios. For any questions related to the protection of your data, you may contact our Data Protection Officer (DPO) via email: cdwebstudios.contact@gmail.com.

2. Data Categories and Purposes

Data collection on this platform occurs exclusively through voluntary action by the User, limited to the following scenarios:

  • Commercial Management and Quotes: When using our form, we collect your name, email, and project details. This data is used strictly to prepare tailored proposals, provide technical consulting, and perform pre-contractual steps.
  • Communication via WhatsApp: Activating the contact button redirects you to WhatsApp. The processing of your phone number serves only to provide an immediate response to your inquiry. The app's infrastructure is governed by Meta Platforms' policies.

3. Legal Basis and Retention

We process your data based on your explicit consent and the need to perform pre-contractual steps. Data will be retained for 24 months after the last recorded contact for commercial history management, or for the legally required period (e.g., billing) if a project agreement is formalized.

4. Data Security and Integrity

As experts in web infrastructure, we apply rigorous security protocols to protect your data. We use encrypted connections (SSL/HTTPS), strict access control to our servers, and defense systems against web vulnerabilities, ensuring the integrity and confidentiality of the information shared with us.

5. Third-Party Processors and Transfers

C&D Web Studios does not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes. Your data may only be accessed by strictly necessary technological infrastructure providers (e.g., secure web hosting, email platforms), who act as data processors under the GDPR, guaranteeing the same level of protection.

6. Data Subject Rights

In accordance with European legislation, the User has total control over their data and may exercise the following rights at any time:

  • Access and Rectification: Request to view or correct your data.
  • Erasure: Demand the deletion of your records ("right to be forgotten").
  • Objection and Portability: Object to certain processing or request the transfer of your data.
  • Complaint to the Supervisory Authority: If you believe there is a violation, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the National Data Protection Commission (CNPD) in Portugal.

Exercising Your Rights: To enforce any of your legal rights or request clarification regarding our privacy architecture, please contact us at cdwebstudios.contact@gmail.com.
