COOKIE AND DIGITAL TRACKING POLICY

REGULATORY COMPLIANCE (GDPR) | LAST REVISED: JUNE 2026

This policy comprehensively and transparently describes the use of temporary data storage files, commonly known as "Cookies", on the digital platform managed by C&D Web Studios, in strict compliance with European (e-Privacy Directive) and national data protection standards.

Article 1 - Definition and Technological Nature

1. Cookies are small text files containing alphanumeric character strings that are downloaded and stored on the User's electronic terminal (computer, smartphone, or tablet) through the web browser when visiting the website.

2. These identifiers allow us to optimize the browsing experience, retaining information related to User preferences and data sessions, safeguarding the integrity of interactions on the platform, and enabling statistical performance analysis.

Article 2 - Types of Cookies Used

C&D Web Studios restricts the use of trackers to what is strictly necessary to ensure a smooth user experience and optimized operational management, categorizing them as follows:

Category Legal and Technical Purpose Duration
Strictly Necessary Ensure navigation stability, the proper loading of the site's visual architecture, and the secure submission of information. Without these cookies, the site cannot function properly. Session
Functional / Preferences Retain choices made by the user, namely in the language selector (PT/EN) and the acceptance of the cookie banner itself, preventing continuous reconfiguration. Up to 1 year
Analytical and Performance Collect aggregated and anonymous data about website traffic (e.g., most visited pages, time spent, origin of click) for continuous improvement of the digital structure. Variable

Article 3 - Management, Control, and Revocation

1. The User has the sovereign right to configure, block, or delete the reception of cookies at any time through their browser's privacy settings. We warn that fully disabling cookies may severely limit your experience on the platform.

2. To manage your preferences, consult your browser's official instructions:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies and other site data.
  • Safari (Apple): Preferences > Privacy > Block all cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options > Privacy & Security > Enhanced Tracking Protection.

Article 4 - Third-Party Redirects

This website integrates immediate redirection systems to external corporate services, namely the WhatsApp Business API and portfolio links. C&D Web Studios is exempt from technical liability regarding the tracking routines installed on the destination servers of these third-party entities.

Auditing and Specialized Contact: To exercise objection rights or seek clarification regarding technical incidents related to cookie management, users may contact our Data Protection Officer at: cdwebstudios.contact@gmail.com.
